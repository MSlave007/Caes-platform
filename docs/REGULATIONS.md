# CAES Energy Certificate Regulations (BOE Digest)

## Key BOE Requirements

### Energy Savings Qualification
- **Minimum Savings:** 20% of baseline consumption (non-negotiable)
- **Formula:** Savings % = (Old_kWh - New_kWh) / Old_kWh × 100
- **Standard Rate:** 0.086 € per MJ/H (fixed, per BOE directive)

### Installer Commission Caps
- **Maximum:** 30% of total calculated savings (cannot exceed)
- **Minimum:** No minimum, installer can choose 0%
- **Proof:** Must be documented in CAES agreement

### Required Documents (Ficha for Aerotermia)
1. Invoice of new water heater (with all specs visible)
2. Thermal installation certificate (RITE) - before photo
3. Installation certificate (new system)
4. **3 Photographic Proofs:**
   - Serial number visible
   - Heater label/model
   - Water tank connection (show 0.42 reduction factor)
5. ID Card of installer + client
6. Technical datasheet of new heater

### Timeline
- **Installer submits:** All documents required
- **Agency review:** 5-10 business days (recommended)
- **Agency approval:** Legal from timestamp forward
- **Certificate validity:** 10 years from approval date

### Penalties for Non-Compliance
- **Missing documents:** Cannot generate certificate
- **Below 20% savings:** Not eligible (unless manually overridden with justification)
- **Margin > 30%:** Invalid CAES agreement (must re-sign with correct %)
- **False data:** Subject to investigation + fines
